# ADIOS Hospital Management System (HMS)
Planned enterprise suite module.

## Core Modules
- Patient Registration, OPD & IPD admissions
- Doctor Rosters, Appointment Scheduling & Queue
- Bed Management & Ward allocations
- Pharmacy & Prescription Management
- Hospital Billing & Insurance Claims

Inherits UI, tabs, theme engine, and rolling SQLite backups from `packages/adios-core`.
