# ADIOS Point of Sale & GST Billing
Planned enterprise suite module.

## Core Modules
- Fast Barcode POS Billing
- GST Invoicing (B2B, B2C, E-Way Bill export)
- Real-time Inventory & Stock alerts
- Vendor Ledger & Accounts Receivable/Payable
- Thermal 80mm & A4 Invoice printing

Inherits UI, tabs, theme engine, and rolling SQLite backups from `packages/adios-core`.
