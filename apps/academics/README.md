# ADIOS Academics & School Management
Planned enterprise suite module.

## Core Modules
- Student Admissions & Document Locker
- Daily Student & Staff Attendance
- Fee Management, Due Alerts & Printed Receipts
- Timetable & Exam Marksheets
- Notice Board & SMS/Email broadcasts

Inherits UI, tabs, theme engine, and rolling SQLite backups from `packages/adios-core`.
